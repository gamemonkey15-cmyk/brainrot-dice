How do these virtual dice work?

These virtual dice randomly choose a color each time you click the Go Again! button. Each die can land on Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue or Purple (unless you exclude some using the checkboxes on the right). Choose how many dice to roll and change the background color to customize your experience.

About Us

Welcome to Roll Color Dice! This is a fun and simple way to roll colored dice for games and challenges. Choose your dice, roll them, and use the colors for your next adventure!

Exclude Colors

Check a box to stop that color from appearing on the next roll.
