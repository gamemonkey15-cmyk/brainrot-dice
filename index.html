Possible Colors

Possible colors are: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Purple.

Dice History

How do these virtual dice work?

These virtual dice randomly choose a color each time you click the Go Again button. Each die can land on Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue or Purple. You can choose how many dice to roll and change the background color to customize your experience.

About us

Welcome to Roll Color Dice! This website is a fun and simple way to roll colored dice for games and challenges. Choose your dice, roll them, and use the colors for your next adventure.
